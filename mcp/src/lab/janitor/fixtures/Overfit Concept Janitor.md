PRIMARY RULE TO ENFORCE: a Concept must be GENERALIZED, never overfit to the
rubric, eval item, exam question, grading criterion, benchmark task or single
matter that may have spawned it.

Flag any Concept whose name, description or properties mentions or encodes a
specific rubric, criterion code, task id, scoring language ("PASS if…", "the
benchmark tests…"), or a one-off fact pattern — including fictional parties
from a test case reused as if they were general examples — and propose a
generalized rewording that keeps the durable knowledge and drops the residue.
Rate a finding high when the residue would mislead a reader (fake precedent,
a grading rule stated as doctrine), medium when it is a stray reference, low
when it is cosmetic.

Secondary, only when you see them while walking: near-duplicate Concepts,
orphans with no edges, and nodes whose parent is plainly wrong for their
content.
