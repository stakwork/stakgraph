Overview-first: a session with hundreds of turns collapses to its story — who ran (each lane is one AgentSession; click an agent to see the prompt it was handed, its chain's user_input turn 0), and what it did to knowledge (amber concept actions never collapse). Everything between folds into one (N turns) pill — tool calls, results, and the agent's reasoning text alike; click to unroll that slice of the chain, click again to fold it back.

The pill is honest about the data: it spans a contiguous run of Turn nodes (order 14–121 in its tooltip), so expanding is just fetching that slice of the NEXT-chain. Live mode appends to the bottom pill in place — the count ticks up without the page growing.
